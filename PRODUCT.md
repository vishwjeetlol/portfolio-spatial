## Platform

web

## Users
Creative directors, agency founders, luxury and cultural brand commissioners, design studio leaders, and creative technologists seeking high-caliber spatial web development, custom GLSL shaders, 3D interactive experiences, and distinctive digital identity.

## Product Purpose
A flagship personal exhibition and interactive laboratory suite proving Vishwjeet Chavan's creative engineering and spatial design practice through 12 live, uncompromised real-time systems, 3D kinetic stages, and cultural sensory storytelling.

## Positioning
"The browser is a creative medium. Not a filing cabinet." The antithesis of sterile, templated SaaS portfolios—merging vibrant Indian street maximalism and aesthetic Jugaad with rigorous computer graphics algorithms running locked at 60 FPS.

## Operating Context
Evaluated on desktop displays with mouse inertia, mobile viewports with touch drag, and studio presentation screens. Visitors explore interactive laboratories, audition procedural audio synthesizers, mint custom postal stamps, and inspect architectural code breakdowns.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 12 original creative coding laboratories (GLSL fluids, 3D rigid-body impulses, GPGPU curl particles, volumetric raymarched SDFs, vinyl turntable DSP, Verlet cloth solver, headless Blender caustics).
- 3D Apple Coverplay carousel with Indian city postal philately and real-time photo booth.
- Full-bleed 3D titanium/glass gyroscope hero stage reacting to pointer physics and audio transients.
- Sub-second production build (390ms with Vite and TypeScript).
- Performance budget: locked 60 FPS (16.6ms frame budget), zero console errors.
