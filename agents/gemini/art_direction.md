# Spatial Studio Art Direction Guide

## 1. Visual Language & Spatial Materiality
- **Spatial Substrate:** Dark cosmic silver environment (`#0a0b0e` to `#12151c`).
- **Frosted Glass:** Multi-surface diffusion with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)` and `-webkit-backdrop-filter`.
- **Rims & Borders:** Ultra-subtle 1px brushed silver gradients (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` top to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` bottom).
- **Hero Centerpiece:** Refractive optical icosphere suspended inside three nested brushed-titanium gimbal rings.

## 2. Typography
- **Stack:** Strict system font hierarchy without external webfont latency:
  `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "SF Pro Text", system-ui, sans-serif`.
- **Monospace Accent:** `"SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace` for data badges and telemetry.

## 3. Motion Dynamics
- **Kinetic Physics:** Damped spring motion for hover tilt, magnetic dock snapping, and inertia gyro spin.
- **Micro-interactions:** 120ms ease-out transitions on buttons; 300ms cubic-bezier spring on door modals.
